# AI Video Creation Intern

**Remote | 4 Months | 6 Hours/Day, 6 Days/Week | Unpaid Internship**

## About the Role

We are looking for an **AI Video Creation Intern** to help transform INFINEO's story scripts into engaging visual and animated content for children aged 5–12.

You will work across AI image generation, animation, storyboarding, and video assembly while maintaining visual continuity across scenes.

## What You'll Do

- Convert story scripts into visual storyboards and scene sequences
- Create AI-generated visuals and animated scenes for storytelling content
- Maintain consistency in characters, costumes, environments, and visual style across scenes
- Write and refine prompts to achieve the required visual output
- Assemble and refine complete video sequences
- Review outputs for visual errors and continuity issues

## What We're Looking For

- Strong interest in **AI, animation, and visual storytelling**
- Ability to follow detailed instructions and structured workflows
- Willingness to experiment, learn new tools, and iterate on outputs
- Strong attention to detail and visual consistency
- Ability to work independently and meet deadlines

### Good to Have

- Basic experience with AI image or video generation tools
- Experience with Canva, Google Flow, CapCut, Premiere Pro, or similar creative/video tools
- Familiarity with prompt writing or AI-assisted content creation

## What You'll Get

- Certificate of Internship
- Letter of Recommendation (LOR), based on performance
- LinkedIn Recommendation, based on performance
- Hands-on experience with real-world AI video production
- Exposure to AI-powered storytelling and character consistency workflows
- Opportunity to work on content used in children's learning
- High-performing interns may be considered for future full-time opportunities based on performance and business requirements
