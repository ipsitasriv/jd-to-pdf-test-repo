# Founder's Associate Intern

**Remote | 4 Months | 6 Hours/Day, 6 Days/Week | Unpaid Internship**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Role Highlight:** This is a flagship, high-visibility opportunity to work directly alongside the Founder across startup strategy, AI implementation, business operations, and product development. No two weeks look the same—you will be at the center of zero-to-one initiatives that move INFINEO forward.

## About the Role

We are looking for a high-ownership **Founder's Associate Intern** to work directly with the founder on cross-functional projects spanning strategy, AI integration, product experience, and business operations.

In this role, you will act as a force multiplier for the leadership team—tackling high-priority strategic initiatives, implementing AI-driven workflows, optimizing team operations, and turning ambiguous challenges into structured outcomes.

## What You'll Do

- **Direct Founder Support:** Work closely with the founder on high-impact strategic, operational, and growth priorities
- **AI & Workflow Integration:** Build, test, and deploy AI-powered workflows (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) to optimize company-wide efficiency
- **Strategy & Market Research:** Research emerging tools, market trends, competitor landscapes, and new product opportunities
- **Process & Systems Architecture:** Document team workflows, internal operating systems, and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
- **Product & UX QA:** Test products and digital learning experiences to identify usability improvements and product enhancements
- **Cross-Functional Execution:** Coordinate across hiring, operations, curriculum, and creative teams to ensure seamless execution
- **Agile Ownership:** Take immediate ownership of evolving high-priority projects with speed and precision

## What We're Looking For

- **High Ownership & Proactive Mindset:** Ability to take vague problems and figure out solutions independently
- **First-Principles Thinking:** Strong analytical clarity, structured problem-solving, and speed of execution
- **Adaptability:** Comfort operating across diverse, non-repetitive domains with rapidly changing priorities
- **Strong Organizational Ability:** Sharp attention to detail and capability to manage multiple workstreams concurrently
- **Exceptional Communication:** Clear, concise written and verbal articulation

### Good to Have

- Experience leveraging AI productivity & generation tools (**ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Notion AI**)
- Familiarity with Notion, Google Workspace, or modern project management platforms
- Strong passion for edtech, startups, business building, product development, or AI implementation
- Prior leadership, project management, or entrepreneurial initiative experience

- High-performing interns will be fast-tracked for future leadership & full-time roles based on performance and business requirements
