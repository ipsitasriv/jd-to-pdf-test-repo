# Graphic Design Intern

**Remote | 4 Months | 6 Hours/Day, 6 Days/Week | Unpaid Internship**

## About the Role

We are looking for a **Graphic Design Intern** to help transform raw educational content, story narratives, and curriculum outlines into clean, engaging, and visually structured presentation decks and digital graphics using Canva for children aged 5–12 at INFINEO.

You will work on visual storytelling, presentation design, layout structuring, typography hierarchy, and lightweight video graphics to create compelling learning experiences.

## What You'll Do

- Design structured course presentation decks and visual learning materials using **Canva**
- Convert raw lesson text, story outlines, and activities into visually engaging, easy-to-read slide layouts
- Maintain strict visual consistency across brand typography, color palettes, spacing, alignment, and graphic elements
- Utilize iconography, custom visual layouts, and graphic elements to simplify complex concepts for children aged 5–12
- Assist in creating basic video graphics and animated slide content using Canva or lightweight editing software
- Collaborate closely with curriculum and content teams to iterate and refine design outputs based on feedback

## What We're Looking For

- Basic knowledge of **Canva** (required)
- Strong understanding of layout design, visual hierarchy, typography, color harmony, and alignment
- Ability to create clean, modern, and structured visual designs suited for young learners (aged 5–12)
- Fluent written and verbal English communication skills
- High attention to detail and strong receptiveness to design feedback and iteration

### Good to Have

- Basic video editing skills using Canva, CapCut, or simple editing software
- Interest in visual storytelling, graphic design, EdTech, or children's educational media
- Exposure to AI design generation tools

## What You'll Get

- Certificate of Internship
- Letter of Recommendation (LOR), based on performance
- LinkedIn Recommendation, based on performance
- Hands-on experience designing visual assets for real-world EdTech products
- Exposure to collaborative content-and-design workflows in a fast-paced environment
- Opportunity to work on visual media used in children's learning
- High-performing interns may be considered for future full-time opportunities based on performance and business requirements
