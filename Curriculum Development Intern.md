# Curriculum Development Intern

**Remote | 4 Months | 6 Hours/Day, 6 Days/Week | Unpaid Internship**

## About the Role

We are looking for a **Curriculum Development Intern** to help structure mythology-based learning modules and character-building curricula for children aged 5–12.

You will work on translating story narratives into structured learning materials, extracting timeless values and life lessons, connecting them to modern-day challenges faced by children, and building clear, engaging lesson modules.

## What You'll Do

- Extract core values, emotional intelligence insights, and life lessons from provided story narratives
- Connect story-based concepts to modern-day real-world challenges faced by children (e.g., peer pressure, resilience, decision-making)
- Structure lesson modules into clear learning flows (story → insight → activity → reflection)
- Design structured, visually clean lesson presentations and learning materials to deliver lessons effectively
- Create interactive activities, discussion prompts, and reflection exercises for classroom delivery
- Ensure content is clear, simple, engaging, and age-appropriate for children aged 5–12
- Collaborate with creative and content teams to iterate and refine curriculum materials based on feedback

## What We're Looking For

- Strong ability to structure, organize, and simplify complex information into clear learning paths
- Genuine interest in **storytelling, education, child development, or character building**
- Clear, effective written communication skills with strong logical thinking
- High attention to detail and content organization
- Ability to work independently, take ownership, and iterate based on feedback

### Good to Have

- Experience with **Canva** for visual content and lesson material creation
- Familiarity with AI and creation tools such as **ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google Flow**
- Interest in or familiarity with Indian mythology stories and timeless folklore
- Prior experience in curriculum design, content creation, teaching, or instructional design

## What You'll Get

- Certificate of Internship
- Letter of Recommendation (LOR), based on performance
- LinkedIn Recommendation, based on performance
- Hands-on experience in curriculum development, instructional design, and EdTech content creation
- Exposure to real-world character-building frameworks and story-driven learning systems
- Opportunity to work on content used in children's learning
- High-performing interns may be considered for future full-time opportunities based on performance and business requirements
