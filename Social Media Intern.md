# Social Media Intern

**Remote | 4 Months | 6 Hours/Day, 6 Days/Week | Unpaid Internship**

## About the Role

We are looking for a **Social Media Intern** to build, grow, and manage INFINEO's social media presence across digital platforms (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts) from the ground up.

You will work on creating engaging visual posts, short-form video content (Reels, Shorts), writing sharp captions, managing content calendars, executing daily posting schedules, and refining content strategies based on audience engagement.

## What You'll Do

- Create visually clean, structured social media posts, carousel graphics, and stories using **Canva**
- Produce short-form video content (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts) for educational storytelling and character-building themes
- Write clear, engaging, and age-tailored captions with relevant hashtags and call-to-actions (CTAs)
- Manage and execute a consistent daily posting schedule across social channels to build brand presence from scratch
- Experiment with diverse content formats, visual styles, and trending hooks to increase organic reach and engagement
- Track post metrics, analyze performance insights, and iterate content strategies based on team feedback and data

## What We're Looking For

- Basic to intermediate proficiency in **Canva** (required)
- Ability to create clean, simple, visually appealing, and well-structured media assets
- High execution discipline and commitment to maintaining regular, disciplined posting schedules
- Strong written communication skills for writing engaging, punchy social media captions
- Growth mindset, receptiveness to creative feedback, and willingness to continuously improve content quality

### Good to Have

- Basic short-form video editing skills using Canva, CapCut, Premiere Rush, or mobile editing apps
- Understanding of social media algorithms, viral hooks, and engagement trends
- Interest in children's education, visual storytelling, EdTech, or character building
- Exposure to AI tools (**Canva AI, ChatGPT, Midjourney, Claude**) for content ideas, caption drafting, or prompt generation

## What You'll Get

- Certificate of Internship
- Letter of Recommendation (LOR), based on performance
- LinkedIn Recommendation, based on performance
- Hands-on experience building brand social media channels from scratch in an edtech startup
- Direct exposure to digital content creation, visual marketing, short-form video production, and social growth mechanics
- Opportunity to shape public-facing brand content used in children's learning
- High-performing interns may be considered for future full-time opportunities based on performance and business requirements
